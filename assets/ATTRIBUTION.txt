ORBIT modifications
Individual source paths, MIDI roots, layers and integrity hashes are in
instruments.json. Recordings were downmixed, resampled, DC-filtered, trimmed,
faded, loudness-matched and logarithmically companded. Quiet sustained-note
pre-rolls are trimmed for responsive short hits, retaining a brief natural
attack and a four-millisecond fade without speeding up or pitch-shifting
the recording. Sustained recordings
have crossfaded body loops and release continuations. Metal guitar adds
oversampled soft saturation and cabinet filtering. Plucked double bass
adds source-relative low-cut EQ for the built-in speaker's limited bandwidth.
These changes do not make the recordings proprietary Casio/other keyboard samples.

Electronic presets, demo arrangements and vocal phrases are original ORBIT
material. Vocal phrases are generated locally with Windows speech synthesis
using David, Hazel and Zira and expressive SSML; they are not user recordings.

The original startup and automatic-shutdown melodies in tools\power-chimes\
were rendered with ORBIT's glock preset, using the VSCO 2 Community Edition
glockenspiel recordings credited above (CC0 1.0). Their approved mono 16 kHz
PCM16 WAVs are preserved without resynthesis; tools\build_power_chimes.py
losslessly encodes them for firmware playback.
